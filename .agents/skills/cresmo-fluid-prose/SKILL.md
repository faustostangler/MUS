# Cresmo Fluid Prose Detranscriptor

## Overview

The `cresmo-fluid-prose` skill executes the pure linguistic detranscription and normalization phase of the Cresmo ecosystem. It transforms raw spoken transcripts, lecture recordings, and draft texts into clean, authoritative, continuous Markdown prose written strictly in the third person with a neutral, objective, and analytical tone.

All prose generated by this skill must strictly adhere to the authorial voice and standards codified in the [`cresmo-style-guide`](file:///home/stangler/gamer_d/Fausto%20Stangler/Documentos/Python/PES/.agents/skills/cresmo-style-guide/SKILL.md).

It purges all speech noise, verbal crutches, hesitations, direct audience interactions, oralities, and visual noise (diagrams, tables, ASCII charts, LaTeX, blockquotes, and bullet lists), systematically audits and corrects the spelling of Named Entities (NER), and formats the output exclusively as continuous narrative prose.

---

## Operating Protocol & Execution Rules

1. **Absolute Suppression of Orality, Speech Noise and Formatting Noise**:
   - Expurge timestamps, hesitations, verbal crutches, false starts, filler words, speaker self-references ("i guess", "as said", "people say"), and direct audience addresses.
   - Adopt strictly the third-person singular with a factual, direct, assertive, and neutral senior jornalistic-analyst voice.
   - Strip the input YAML header completely. Do NOT output any YAML frontmatter, headers, tags, or metadata blocks. Zero `---` delimiters anywhere in the document.
   - Complete ban on diagrams, flow charts, ASCII schematics, markdown tables, bullet points (`*`, `-`), numbered lists in the main body, LaTeX blocks, and blockquotes (`>`). Extract all operational mechanics and structural logic into continuous fluid narrative paragraphs.

2. **Compulsory Fact-Checking and Named Entity (NER) Normalization**:
   - Correct phonetic distortions, transcription misspellings, proper names of historical figures, institutions, geographic locations, corporate entities, foundational treatises, and canonical theoretical concepts to standard encyclopedic spelling.
   - Align dates and temporal anchors with Big-Endian notation (YYYY, Century BCE/CE).

3. **Mandatory Heading Hierarchy & Line 1 Contract**:
   - The document MUST begin directly on Line 1 with the first Markdown heading (`##`) representing the initial macro-phase or conceptual era. Never output a level 1 heading (`#`), YAML block, or conversational preamble.
   - Use `##` for Eras, Centuries, or Major Macro Phases.
   - Use `###` for Decades, Years, or Hierarchical Conceptual Blocks.

4. **Cresmo Complimentary Style Guide Compliance (Authorial Voice & Anti-Patterns)**:
   - **Typography**: Bold (`**...**`) strictly and exclusively on the first occurrence of named entities, dates, theoretical concepts, and key terms. Italics (`_..._`) strictly for foreign terms, cultural works, software, and hardware names.
   - **STRICT SYNTAX BANS**: Strictly NEVER EVER use em-dashes (`—`). Replace with commas, parentheses, or distinct sentences. Strictly NEVER EVER use binary contrast formulas or negative antitheses (e.g., "não X, mas Y", "não se trata de X, e sim de Y"). Formulate all statements affirmatively.
