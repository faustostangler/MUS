# Cresmo Wide-Expander (Synchronic Cross-Sections & Axial Time)

## Overview

The `cresmo-wide-expander` skill performs systematic synchronic transversal expansion, comparative analysis, and connected global auditing on conceptual texts.

All prose generated by this skill must strictly comply with the authorial voice and standards codified in the [`cresmo-style-guide`](file:///home/stangler/gamer_d/Fausto%20Stangler/Documentos/Python/PES/.agents/skills/cresmo-style-guide/SKILL.md).

The `cresmo-wide-expander` takes a sharp **synchronic transversal cross-section** across different places at a specific historical moment or epoch to investigate different perspectives. It seeks connections, comparisons, and convergences between apparently separate societies or places, uncovering patterns and independent trajectories.

The `cresmo-wide-expander` operates systematically across two primary analytical axes:
1. **Synchrony and Connectivity (Global/Connected)**: Tracing parallel events that appear isolated on the surface but are bound together by real, often subterranean contact networks, transcontinental routes, monetary flows, disease vectors, or other anomalies.
2. **Parallelism and Analogy (Comparative Analysis & Axial Time)**: Analyzing independent places that underwent strikingly analogous events during the same epoch with zero or minimal contact, responding to convergent pressures.

The `cresmo-wide-expander` enforces strict diagram suppression, complete eradication of oralities and conversational artifacts, and formats its output exclusively as high-density, continuous, hierarchical Markdown prose.

## Synchronic Audit Methodology

Execute an internal 2-pass audit on the input text before composing the final expanded text:

### Pass 1: Intra-Text Horizontal Audit (5 Synchronic Dimensions)
1. **Synchronic Slicing (Where else in the world?)**:
   - When the text describes an event or transition in a specific region at time $T$, what was simultaneously happening elsewhere during that exact same $T$?
2. **Connectivity & Teleconnection Audit**:
   - Were the actors described connected to distant networks of trade, bullion circulation, religious pilgrimage, or intellectual translation? What hidden flows were pulsing underneath, if any?
3. **Planetary Environmental Conditioning**:
   - Did global or hemispheric climatic anomalies (solar minimums, volcanic winters, El Niño-Southern Oscillation episodes, megadroughts) coincide with the crisis or transformation under analysis, if any?
4. **Structural Analogy Verification**:
   - If there are similar solutions with different origins, did they copy one another, or did they independently invent the solution under similar material stresses?
5. **Axial Reflexivity & Cognitive Depth**:
   - Does the phenomenon reflect a moment of spiritual or intellectual transition (an "Axial turning point") where traditional customs collapsed and systematic self-reflection emerged, or just coincidence?

### Pass 2: Comparative Mapping
1. **Trans-Regional Cross-Mapping**: Construct explicit narrative parallels between at least two distinct realms during the same temporal horizon.
2. **Common vs. Isolated Genesis**: Analyze the common factors versus isolated parallel genesis.
3. **Differential Outcomes**: Explain why two realms subjected to the same external shock reacted with different configurations.
