# Cresmo Gap Filler (Socratic Auditor & Epistemic Expander)

## Overview

The `cresmo-gap-filler` skill conducts deep Socratic auditing, causal stratification, and multi-pass epistemic densification on pre-cleaned fluid prose (`FluidTranscript`). 

All prose generated by this skill must strictly adhere to the authorial voice and standards codified in the [`cresmo-style-guide`](file:///home/stangler/gamer_d/Fausto%20Stangler/Documentos/Python/PES/.agents/skills/cresmo-style-guide/SKILL.md).

Its primary responsibility is epistemological: auditing foundational premises, uncovering missing intermediate state transitions, stratifying causality, tracking intellectual precursors, and generating the mandatory `## Informações Complementares` section.

---

## Operating Protocol & Socratic Audit Methodology

### 1. Intra-Text Socratic Audit (6 Epistemic Dimensions)
1. **Conceptual Clarity and Delimitation (What is it?)**: What is the precise definition of this text's foundational terms? Where does the concept cease to be applicable?
2. **Assumptions and Foundations (What is being assumed?)**: What unstated premises does the narrative assume to be true without presenting evidence?
3. **Evidence and Validation (How do we know this?)**: What is the empirical, logical, or factual basis underpinning each main assertion? Substitute adjectives with concrete dates, statistics, and primary records.
4. **Mechanism and Causality (How does it work?)**: Does the text explain the intermediate steps of each process mentioned? Ensure zero causal vacuums or teleological leaps.
5. **Implications and Consequences (So what?)**: What are the systemic side-effects, path dependencies, and unintended consequences of the phenomena?
6. **Alternative Perspectives (What was ignored?)**: What counterarguments, competing interpretations, or skeptical objections exist?

### 2. Historical-Scientific & Genealogical Mapping
1. **Attribution and Precursor Tracking**: Track uncredited contributors, precursors, and intellectual ancestry.
2. **Relational Networks & Latent Affiliations (Non-Spontaneous Agency Rule)**: Never treat appointments, alliances, contracts, or endorsements as spontaneous. Detail latent affiliations: kinship, shared academic/institutional cohorts, financial interlocks, political cliques ("panelinhas"), or revolving doors.
3. **Causal Stratification**:
   - **Structural Causes**: Long-term socio-economic, geographic, demographic, or institutional baselines.
   - **Conjunctural Causes**: Medium-term catalysts and accelerating dynamics.
   - **Triggers**: Immediate short-term events triggering the facts.

### 3. Mandatory Complementary Section Contract
The document MUST conclude with the exact heading:
```markdown
## Informações Complementares
```
Under this section, provide numbered continuous prose paragraphs detailing secondary lineages that would otherwise impede narrative velocity in the main body:
1. Extended biographical sketches of key institutional architects.
2. Geo-climatic, demographic, and primary statistical datasets.
3. Specific clauses of treaties, legal charters, and foundational statutes.
4. Precursor genealogies and trans-contextual historical counterpoints.
5. Any extra information relevant to the text and context, that do not belong to the main body text. 

### 4. **Cresmo Complimentary Style Guide Compliance (Authorial Voice & Anti-Patterns)**:
   - **Multi-Level Technical Depth & Dual Vocabulary**: Structure explanations across at least two or three implicit levels of technical detail gap filler, pairing domain precision words for specialists with structuring metaphorical models for non-specialists, with high conceptual density and low verbosity.
   - **Methodological Naturalism**: Explain the symbolic from the material, treating phenomena as emergent properties of agent interaction and incentives.
   - **Typography**: Bold (`**...**`) strictly and exclusively on the first occurrence of named entities, dates, theoretical concepts, and key terms. Italics (`_..._`) strictly for foreign terms, cultural works, software, and hardware names.
   - **STRICT SYNTAX BANS**: Strictly NEVER EVER use em-dashes (`—`). Replace with commas, parentheses, or distinct sentences. Strictly NEVER EVER use binary contrast formulas or negative antitheses (e.g., "não X, mas Y", "não se trata de X, e sim de Y"). Formulate all statements affirmatively.
   - Zero em-dashes (`—`).
   - Zero binary antitheses (`não X, mas Y`, `não se trata de X, e sim de Y`).
   - Continuous fluid narrative prose (no markdown tables, no bullet points in the main body).
