# Stangler Analyst — Critical Discourse Analysis & Epistemic Purification

Skill for executing **Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA / ACD)**, **Epistemic Fact Purification**, and **Multidimensional Opinion Categorization** on textual transcripts, media broadcasts, political columns, and articles.

---

## Theoretical Frameworks

This skill integrates 11 fundamental theoretical frameworks across 5 dimensions:

1. **George Lakoff (Cognitive Framing & Reframing):**
   - *Framework:* Frame Theory (*Don't Think of an Elephant!* & *Metaphors We Live By*).
   - *Application:* Golden Rule of Reframing ("Never negate the opponent's frame using their vocabulary"). Identifies neural metaphor structures and establishes independent cognitive framing.
2. **Teun van Dijk (Social Cognition, Ideology & CDA):**
   - *Framework:* Socio-cognitive model (*Discurso e Poder*, *Ideologia*).
   - *Application:* Unmasks US vs. THEM polarization, mental affect models, and **Ventriloquized Discourse** (projecting opinions through metonymies like "the market", "allies", "sources").
3. **Norman Fairclough (Three-Dimensional CDA):**
   - *Framework:* 3D Model (*Text, Discursive Practice, Social Practice*).
   - *Application:* Analyzes epistemic/deontic modality, order of discourse, and naturalization of economic/political hegemony.
4. **Laurence Bardin (Content Analysis):**
   - *Framework:* Categorization & Topography (*Análise de Conteúdo*).
   - *Application:* Codification, qualitative/quantitative categorization, and discursive topography (primacy and recency effects in text layout).
5. **J.R. Martin & P.R.R. White (Appraisal System):**
   - *Framework:* Attitude (Affect, Judgment, Appreciation), Engagement, Graduation (*The Language of Evaluation*).
   - *Application:* Evaluative language taxonomy for mapping emotional projections, capacity/moral judgments, and aesthetic appreciation.
6. **Mikhail Bakhtin, Henri Bergson & Sigmund Freud (Humor, Satire & Carnivalesque):**
   - *Framework:* Bergson (*Laughter* - mechanical rigidity over life), Freud (*Jokes and Their Relation to the Unconscious* - hostile jokes/reductive laughter), Bakhtin (*Carnivalization*).
   - *Application:* Analyzes how irony and satire act as tactical delegitimization tools that bypass policy debate.
7. **John Searle & F.R. Palmer (Speech Acts & Modality):**
   - *Framework:* Speech Acts (Assertive, Directive, Expressive, Declarative) & Modality (Epistemic & Deontic).
   - *Application:* Detects **Epistemic Determinism** ("closing the future") and deontic prescriptions masked as tactical analysis.
8. **William McGuire (Inoculation Theory):**
   - *Framework:* Psychological Inoculation (*Attitude Change via Inoculation*).
   - *Application:* Pre-exposure to attenuated narrative variants to build psychological immunity against media manipulation.
9. **Sun Tzu & Carl von Clausewitz (Narrative Warfare):**
   - *Framework:* Indirect Maneuver & Semiotic Friction (*The Art of War*, *On War*).
   - *Application:* Concrete vs. Abstract Agenda maneuvers and refusal of opponent-chosen battlegrounds.
10. **Pierre Bourdieu (Sociology of the Journalistic Field):**
    - *Framework:* Capital Exchange & Habitus (*On Television*, *The Journalistic Field*).
    - *Application:* Explains the symbiotic exchange between columnists and elite sources (scoops for strategic visibility/trial balloons).
11. **Edward S. Herman & Noam Chomsky (Propaganda Model):**
    - *Framework:* Mass Media Filters (*Manufacturing Consent*).
    - *Application:* Demonstrates how official/corporate sources set the dominant media framing.

## Core Analytical Protocols

### Protocol 1: Epistemic Fact Purification (Filtro Desacoplado / Filter De-coupling)

When given a text or transcript, decouple empirical data from adjectival/emotional noise:

1. **Extract Core Empirical Data:**
   - Numerical metrics, dates, official acts, legislative votes, verified legal proceedings, physical events, explicit direct quotes.
2. **Strip Adjectival & Emotional Noise:**
   - Remove dramatics (*"tropeço dramático"*, *"pânico"*, *"sangramento"*, *"desespero"*).
3. **Formulate Purified Standalone Facts:**
   - Write neutral, objective statements representing only what is empirically verifiable.

---

### Protocol 2: Multidimensional Opinion Categorization

Categorize every non-factual statement into the **6 Standard Opinion Categories**:

| Category | Definition | Key Theoretical Markers | Typical Examples |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Opinião Ideológica (Normativa / Hegemônica)** | Naturalization of class/market interests as universal truth. | ACD (Fairclough/van Dijk), Appraisal (Proclamation/Endorse). | *"A Faria Lima já aceitou X como única alternativa..."* |
| **2. Opinião Humorística, Irônica e Satírica (Lúdica / Desqualificadora)** | Use of irony, satire, and ridicule to delegitimize without debate. | Bergson (Rigidez Mecânica), Freud (Chiste Hostil), Bakhtin (Carnavalização). | *"Novela mexicana encenada"*, *"papel de bobo"*, *"chá de cadeira"*. |
| **3. Opinião Avaliativa Moral (Caráter e Competência)** | Judgment of social capacity, integrity, or sincerity. | Appraisal (Attitude -> Judgment), Searle (Atos Expressivos). | *"Escolhe uma mulher por puro desespero"*, *"pirraça institucional"*. |
| **4. Opinião Afetiva e Emocional (Estados de Espírito)** | Imputation of psychological states (panic, anxiety, relief). | Appraisal (Attitude -> Affect), van Dijk (Modelos Mentais). | *"Clima de pânico no QG"*, *"de cara amarrada"*. |
| **5. Opinião Especulativa e Determinista (Epistêmica)** | Transforming rumors/polls into absolute future certainties ("cerrar o futuro"). | Palmer (Modalidade Epistêmica), Searle (Assertivos Especulativos). | *"Não há a menor chance de X"*, *"Lula é o favorito inevitável"*. |
| **6. Opinião Prescritiva / Deôntica (Orientação de Conduta)** | Advising or dictating what an actor "must" do. | Palmer (Modalidade Deôntica), Searle (Atos Diretivos). | *"Flávio tem que correr bastante"*, *"É preciso reciclar"*. |

---

### Protocol 3: Sociolinguistic & Critical Discourse Analysis (ACD)

Analyze the structural layout and discourse mechanics:

1. **Discursive Topography (Bardin):**
   - *Primacy Effect (Opening):* How headlines and lead paragraphs set emotional expectations.
   - *Recency Effect (Closing):* How closing remarks leave a lasting moral or fatalistic impression.
2. **Ventriloquism & Source Attribution (van Dijk & Bourdieu):**
   - Unmask metonymies (*"O mercado avalia"*, *"Aliados dizem"*, *"Bastidores indicam"*). Identify the concrete interested actor behind the source and apply *Cui bono?* (Who benefits?).
3. **Valence & Polarization Gradients:**
   - Map US vs. THEM alignments, positive self-presentation vs. negative opponent presentation.

---

### Protocol 4: Cognitive Immunity & Counter-Narrative Manual

Formulate defensive and offensive counter-narrative strategies:

1. **Golden Rule (Lakoff):** Never adopt the opponent's vocabulary or framed battleground.
2. **Lexical Substitution:** Replace loaded terms with neutral or counter-framed terms.
3. **Psychological Inoculation (McGuire):** Expose audiences to pre-bunked, weakened versions of manipulative tropes.
4. **Concrete vs. Abstract Agenda Maneuver (Sun Tzu / Clausewitz):** Shift debate from abstract emotional gossips (*"clima de pânico"*) to concrete material data (*"investimentos, empregos, obras"*).

## Guidelines & Best Practices

1. **Maintain Strict Epistemic Rigor:** Never classify a speculative assertion or anonymous quote as a fact.
2. **Always Code in English when writing scripts/tools, write analytical text in Portuguese/English as requested by the user.**
3. **Use GitHub-style Alerts and Diagrams:** Include Mermaid diagrams for context maps, theoretical flows, and comparative matrices.
4. **Preserve Exact Quotes:** When identifying opinions, always provide exact text citations from the source transcript.
